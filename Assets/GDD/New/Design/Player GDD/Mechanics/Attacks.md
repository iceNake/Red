The player will be able to use many attacks but will mainly be divided into red and grandma attacks.

red attacks will be used with attach button 1(input manager)  and will do a different attack depending on the directional input. both left and right directional input will do the same attack but to each side respectively. pressing attack button 1 without any directional input will result in a "jab combo". air attacks will result in new attacks as well.

Ground Attacks
- neutral - Jab (static, combo)
- directional L/R - Lunge (traversal, ends combo)
- directional Up - Uppercut (traversal, lengthens combo)
- directional Down - Sweep (static, combo)

Aereal Attacks
- neutral - Swing (static)
- directional L/R - Launch (traversal)
- directional Up - Spin (traversal)
- directional Down - Woodcutter (traversal)
